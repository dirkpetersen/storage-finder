Where should your files and research data live?

Storage Finder compares the storage services available to you (Box, OneDrive, SharePoint, shared drives, Tier 1, 2 and 3 research storage, and more) against the sensitivity of your data, who needs access, how much you have, whether it needs a backup, and whether you want to stay within a free quota or pay for capacity.

Simple

Browse every service, tick the ones you like, and compare them.

  • One card per service, with the attributes people search for
  • Search by name or feature, or narrow the cards with quick filters
  • Select two or more and choose Compare for a detailed side-by-side table
Browse services Best when you want to see what exists.

Select

Say what you need and see what fits.

  • Pick your data classification, who needs access, size, backup, use and cost
  • Services that cannot work drop out, and choices that lead nowhere are grayed out
  • What remains lines up in columns
Open Select Best when you know your requirements.

Guided wizard

Answer six questions and get a ranked recommendation.

  • One question at a time, with examples to help you decide
  • A best match, other good options, and a plain-language reason for each
  • A link you can share and a print view for your records
Start the wizard Best when you are not sure where to start.

How your data's classification decides what you can use

Every service is approved for some classification levels and not others. All three tools use these three levels to rule out services that are not allowed to hold your data.

This guide helps you choose; it is not a policy decision. Check with your IT support before storing sensitive or confidential data.
