Where should your files and research data live?

Storage Finder compares the storage services available to you (Box, OneDrive, SharePoint, shared drives, Tier 1, 2 and 3 research storage, and more) against the sensitivity of your data, who needs access, how much you have, and whether it needs a backup.

Simple finder

Pick what applies, then compare services side by side.

  • Choose tiles in any order, or only the ones you care about
  • Services that cannot work are removed and choices that lead nowhere are grayed out
  • Every remaining service lines up in a column: cost, capacity, backup, allowed data
Open the simple finder Best when you already know roughly what you need.

Guided wizard

Answer six questions and get a ranked recommendation.

  • One question at a time, with examples to help you decide
  • A best match, other good options, and a plain-language reason for each
  • A link you can share and a print view for your records
Start the wizard Best when you are not sure where to start.

How your data's classification decides what you can use

Every service is approved for some classification levels and not others. Both tools use these three levels to rule out services that are not allowed to hold your data.

This guide helps you choose; it is not a policy decision. Check with your IT support before storing sensitive or confidential data.
